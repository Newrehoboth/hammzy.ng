## 📈 Tracking Your SEO Success

Monitor these metrics in Google Analytics:
1. **Organic Traffic**: Visitors from Google search (aim for growth)
2. **Keyword Rankings**: Position for target keywords (aim for top 10)
3. **Backlinks**: Number of sites linking to you (aim for 20+ quality links)
4. **Click-Through Rate**: Percentage of people who click your result (aim for >3%)
5. **Bounce Rate**: People who leave immediately (aim for <50%)
6. **Average Session Duration**: Time spent on site (aim for >2 minutes)
7. **Pages Per Session**: Number of pages visited (aim for >2)
